Why Support Us

The Lasting Impact of Your Giving

At Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc., every dollar is stewarded with purpose, integrity, and faith. When you invest in our mission, you invest directly in our local neighbors.

We serve youth seeking a sense of identity and positive role models, seniors needing assistance and human connection, and families working through emotional, financial, or relational hardship. We believe transformation doesn't happen in isolation — it happens when a community stands together.

✓
Guiding Youth & Teens:

Providing positive mentorship, self-worth development, conflict resolution, and leadership skills for young men and women.

✓
Family Stabilization & Guidance:

Equipping families with emotional support, guidance resources, and community connections to rebuild strength and stability.

✓
Senior Dignity & Care:

Ensuring older adults in our area receive companionship, wellness check-ins, respect, and tangible support.

✓
Faith-Rooted Kingdom Impact:

Walking out the biblical vision to "write the vision and make it plain" (Habakkuk 2:2), bringing unconditional dignity to all.

"Write the vision and make it plain, that he may run who reads it."

Volunteer Opportunities

Whether you can assist for two hours a week, participate in weekend outreach events, or facilitate a workshop, we have a place for your unique gifts:

Youth Guidance & Mentorship

Youth

Senior Companionship & Care

Seniors

Provide telephone wellness check-ins, friendly conversation, holiday card delivery, and community support for seniors.

Community Outreach & Events

Outreach

Administrative & Professional Support

Operations
