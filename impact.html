Accountability & Stewardship

Our Commitment to Transparency

Trust is the bedrock of community service. We believe in clear accountability, ethical leadership, and open communication with the neighbors and donors who make our mission possible.

Our Guiding Standards

How We Uphold Your Trust

Every contribution of time, resource, or financial support is treated as a sacred trust.

IRS 501(c)(3)

Official Tax-Exempt Status

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. is an authorized 501(c)(3) public charity. Charitable contributions to SYS are tax-deductible to the fullest extent permitted by federal and Georgia state law.

Program Focus

Mission-Directed Resources

We prioritize direct human services — mentorship for youth, practical support for struggling families, and compassionate outreach for local seniors — minimizing excessive administrative overhead.

Public Disclosure

Annual Reporting & Form 990

In accordance with federal regulations, our annual financial reports and IRS Form 990 filings will be made available upon request and through standard nonprofit public transparency directories.

Real Human Impact

Measuring What Truly Matters

We do not measure our work with manufactured statistics. We measure success in transformed lives, restored dignity, and families finding a safe anchor.

When an adolescent regains confidence through mentorship, when an elderly neighbor receives companionship, or when a family receives practical guidance during an emotional crisis — that is the heartbeat of Sincerely You Are Significant.
