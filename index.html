Our Mission

A Safe Space for Every
Stage of Life

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. is a 501(c)(3) human services nonprofit dedicated to serving youth, seniors, families, and individuals who need support, structure, guidance, and a safe place to heal.

We believe that every person — regardless of their circumstances — carries inherent dignity and profound worth. Through compassionate outreach, dedicated programming, and community partnership, we walk alongside those who need us most.

Our Core Values

What We Stand For

Four pillars guide everything we do — from the people we serve to the partnerships we build and the community we create.

Empowering Individuals

Every person has unique gifts, potential, and purpose. We provide tools, resources, and encouragement to help each individual discover and live out their significance.

Strengthening Families

Families are the foundation of healthy communities. We support families through challenges and equip them to thrive with stability, connection, and renewed hope.

Building Community

True transformation happens in relationship. We cultivate spaces where people gather, connect, support one another, and build a stronger community together.

Advancing Kingdom Impact

Rooted in faith and guided by purpose, we pursue meaningful, lasting impact — honoring the vision written plainly for all who dare to run with it.

Bianca Reid Founder & Provider
Meet Our Founder
Bianca Reid

Leading with Faith,
Purpose & Possibility

Bianca Reid is the Founder and Provider of Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. — a human services nonprofit built on the convictions of Faith, People, Families, Community, and Impact.

Driven by a vision to see every person walk in their God-given significance, Bianca established SYS as a safe harbor for those who need support, structure, and genuine community. Her mission is rooted in the belief that People, Purpose, and Possibility are within reach for everyone.
