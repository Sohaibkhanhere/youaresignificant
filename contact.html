Please do not enter sensitive medical history, formal psychiatric diagnoses, or emergency requests into this web form. How to Schedule Your In-Person Consultation

To maintain confidentiality, personal dignity, and dedicated focus for every individual and family we serve, all consultations at our Jonesboro office are pre-scheduled. Follow these simple steps to book your visit.

1

Call or Inquire

Call our intake coordinator at 404-997-2563 or email admin@youaresignificant.org to express interest in youth, senior, or family services.

2

Intake Screening

We conduct a brief, supportive phone conversation to understand your needs, answer any questions, and ensure our programs align with your specific situation.

3

Date & Time Confirmation

Once scheduled, you will receive full arrival details, including directions, parking information, and check-in guidance for our Jonesboro office.

4

Dedicated Consultation

Meet one-on-one with our provider or program staff in a warm, confidential, and safe setting designed to provide structure, hope, and real solutions.

Strict Pre-Scheduled Appointment Policy

Because we respect our clients' privacy and time, our Jonesboro office does not accept unscheduled walk-ins. Staff members may be conducting confidential sessions, running group mentorship cohorts, or participating in off-site community programs. Please call ahead at 404-997-2563 so that we can give you our undivided attention.

Emergency & Crisis Resources

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. is a human services non-profit agency providing support by appointment. SYS does NOT provide 24/7 emergency response, inpatient psychiatric treatment, or crisis crisis shelter. If you or someone you care about is experiencing an immediate crisis or is in physical danger, please utilize these verified, 24/7 resources:

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Free, confidential, 24/7 support for anyone experiencing suicidal thoughts, emotional distress, or mental health-related substance crises.
