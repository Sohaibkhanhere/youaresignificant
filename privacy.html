Legal & Transparency

Privacy Policy & Accessibility

We believe transparency, dignity, and respect are fundamental to everything we do. Learn how we handle your information and our commitment to an accessible digital experience.

Last updated: January 2025

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. ("SYS," "we," "us," or "our") is a 501(c)(3) human services nonprofit agency headquartered in Stockbridge, Georgia. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal information of everyone who interacts with our organization, visits our website (youaresignificant.org), or participates in our programs.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect personal information that you knowingly and voluntarily choose to provide to us. When you communicate with Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. through our website, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Contact Information: Your full name, email address, telephone number, and mailing address when you reach out via our contact or volunteer forms, email us, or call our office.
  • Inquiry Category & Communication Details: The topic or area of assistance requested (e.g., Youth Guidance, Family Support, Senior Care, Volunteer Interest, General Inquiries), along with the narrative text of your message or request.
  • Voluntary Demographic or Scheduling Information: Details you share to help us better serve you, such as preferred appointment times, program interests, or availability for volunteer coordination.
  • Technical and Server Log Information: Standard, non-personally identifiable server log data automatically transmitted by your web browser, including your IP address, browser type, device information, and pages visited, utilized strictly for website maintenance, operational performance, and cyber-security protection.

We do not engage in invasive behavioral tracking, nor do we deploy commercial advertising cookies on this website.

2. How We Use Information

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. uses the information you provide exclusively to carry out our charitable mission, provide compassionate human services, and maintain organizational operations. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Respond to Inquiries: Promptly follow up on your questions, requests for support, or appointment scheduling at our Jonesboro location or Stockbridge administrative office.
  • Deliver and Improve Services: Coordinate youth mentorship sessions, senior assistance outreach, and family support resources, continually refining our programs to best serve community needs.
  • Coordinate Volunteers and Supporters: Onboard prospective volunteers, communicate event schedules, and facilitate community outreach initiatives.
  • Provide Giving Acknowledgment: Verify charitable contributions, issue formal 501(c)(3) tax receipts, and maintain proper accounting and financial records.
  • Protect Organizational Integrity: Detect, prevent, and respond to security threats, fraudulent behavior, or unauthorized website misuse.

3. We Do Not Sell Information

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. has never sold, rented, leased, traded, or monetized personal information, and we never will.

We treat your personal details as a sacred trust. Your contact information is never shared with commercial telemarketers, third-party advertisers, or data brokers. We will only share or disclose information under the following limited circumstances:

  • With Your Direct Consent: When you have explicitly authorized us to connect you with an external community partner or county social service provider for specialized assistance.
  • Trusted Technical Service Providers: With vetted vendors who provide technical infrastructure (such as website hosting, email distribution, or payment processing), bound by confidentiality agreements to use your data solely for supporting our operations.
  • Legal Requirements & Safety: When required by federal, state, or local law, subpoena, or legal process, or when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect someone's physical safety or prevent harm.

4. Contact Form Data & Information Transmission

When you submit an inquiry through our contact or volunteer forms, your message is securely transmitted to our administrative coordination staff to allow us to respond to your request.

We do not sell, rent, or lease your contact information to commercial third parties. Inquiries are handled strictly by authorized personnel to coordinate program consultations, volunteer orientations, or respond to questions.

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect all information sent to us electronically; however, no internet transmission is ever 100% immune from security risks. We urge you to avoid submitting sensitive medical, financial, or confidential crisis records through standard web contact forms.

5. Third-Party Links & Social Media Platforms

Our website provides links to external third-party platforms to help you connect with our community and stay updated on events. These include:

  • Instagram: @theSYSorg
  • Facebook: Sincerely You Are Significant on Facebook
  • External educational, community, or government resource links referenced in our programs.

When you click an external link, you leave youaresignificant.org and are governed by the privacy policies, terms, and data collection practices of that third party. Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. does not control and is not liable for the privacy practices or content of third-party platforms.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Protecting the privacy and well-being of young people is central to our mission as a human services organization. While Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. proudly offers youth guidance, teen mentorship, and family programming, this website is intended for general audiences, adults, parents, and legal guardians.

In compliance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), we do not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information directly from children under the age of 13 through online website forms without verifiable parental or guardian consent.

All youth enrollment, mentorship participation, and program registrations are handled directly in consultation with parents or legal guardians. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child under 13 has submitted personal information through this website, please contact us immediately at admin@youaresignificant.org so that we may promptly remove the record from our systems.

Accessibility Commitment

7. Accessibility Statement

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. is passionately committed to ensuring that our digital resources, communications, and website are fully accessible to individuals of all abilities, including individuals with disabilities.

We strive to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA standards developed by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines outline best practices for creating digital content that is perceivable, operable, understandable, and robust.

Accessibility Features on This Website:

  • Contrast & Legibility: Carefully calibrated color palettes that meet or exceed WCAG AA contrast ratios for readable typography across all device screens.
  • Semantic Structure: Proper HTML5 landmarks (`<header>`, `<nav>`, `<main>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`) and logical heading hierarchies to facilitate screen reader navigation.
  • Keyboard Accessibility: Clear, visible focus indicators for all interactive elements, supporting visitors who navigate without a mouse.
  • Accessible Form Controls: Explicit form field labels, intuitive tab navigation, and informative validation messages.
  • Descriptive Imagery: Thoughtfully crafted alternative text (alt text) for photos and graphics depicting our community work.
  • Reduced Motion Support: Full support for the CSS `prefers-reduced-motion` media query for individuals sensitive to visual animations.

Need Assistance or Encountering a Barrier?

We consider digital accessibility an ongoing priority. If you encounter any accessibility barriers on our website, need assistance accessing any information, or would like to request materials in an alternative format, please reach out to us.
