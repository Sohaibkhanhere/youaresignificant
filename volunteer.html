Lend Your Heart & Talents

Volunteer With Us

Real community transformation is built by caring individuals who choose to show up. Discover how your time, mentorship, and encouragement can change lives across Henry County.

Meaningful Pathways

How You Can Serve

Whether you have two hours a month or regular weekly availability, there is a purposeful place for you on our volunteer team.

Youth Mentorship

Youth Mentor & Ally

Walk alongside adolescents and teens as a trusted guide. Mentors provide encouragement, assist with healthy goal setting, listen without judgment, and reinforce that every youth is inherently significant.

  • 1-on-1 or small group guidance
  • Life-skills & decision-making support
  • Orientation and background screening required
Events & Outreach

Community Event Support

Help bring in-person fellowship days, community care distributions, and youth empowerment workshops to life. Event volunteers assist with hospitality, event setup, registration, and participant welcoming.

  • Hospitality and guest check-in
  • Event day logistics and coordination
  • Flexible, single-day weekend commitments
Life Skills & Advisory

Skills & Resource Partner

Are you a professional, educator, counselor, or craftsperson? Share your expertise through guest workshops, career exploration talks, financial wellness discussions, or resource donations.

  • Career & vocational exploration
  • Family financial basics
  • Creative arts, crafts, and wellness
