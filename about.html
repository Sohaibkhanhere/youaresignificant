About Us

Our Purpose, Our People, Our Promise

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. is an official 501(c)(3) human services nonprofit agency headquartered in Stockbridge, GA. We are dedicated to guiding youth, empowering lives, and building futures through compassionate care, stability, and enduring community support.

Our Mission & Purpose

Who We Are & Who We Serve

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. (also styled as Sincerely You're Significant) is an official 501(c)(3) Human Services Non-Profit Agency. We believe every person — regardless of circumstance — carries inherent worth and profound purpose.

Healing Together. Stronger Together.

Building a Community of Stability, Support, and Hope

Our mission is to walk alongside youth, seniors, families, and individuals who need a compassionate guide, structured support, and a safe place to heal. Based on Eagles Landing Parkway in Stockbridge, GA, with appointment-only services in Jonesboro, GA, SYS is committed to transforming lives through dignity, faith, and purposeful action.

Youth & Teens

Guiding young people through structured mentorship, positive community relationships, and life guidance to help them discover their identity and build a purposeful future.

Guiding Youth

Families

Strengthening the core family unit through compassionate assistance, crisis stabilization, and practical support to build resilient, hopeful households.

Strengthening Futures

Seniors

Honoring our elders with dignified care, meaningful human connection, and compassionate outreach to alleviate isolation and provide trusted companionship.

Compassion in Action

Individuals

Providing a confidential, judgment-free harbor for anyone facing transition or distress, equipping them with guidance, resources, and encouragement to heal and thrive.

Our Approach to Human Services

Compassion in Action, Grounded in Dignity

At Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc., we do not view people through the lens of their struggles. We view them through the lens of their worth, their dignity, and their potential.

Our human services model is holistic and relationship-centered. Whether working with a young person navigating life choices, a family undergoing a difficult transition, or a senior seeking warmth and connection, our provider team is committed to meeting individuals right where they are.

Rooted in our founder convictions of Faith, People, Families, Community, and Impact, we believe wholeheartedly in People, Purpose, and Possibility. Individualized sessions and provider guidance are available by appointment at our Jonesboro office.

How SYS Began
Bianca Reid

Born from Faith, Guided by Purpose

Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. was founded by Bianca Reid (Founder & Provider) out of an enduring desire to ensure that no individual or family feels forgotten, invisible, or without hope.

Recognizing the critical need across Stockbridge, Jonesboro, and surrounding Georgia communities for compassionate human services that combine practical support with deep emotional and spiritual encouragement, Bianca stepped out to create a safe haven. She established SYS on the unwavering truth that every human soul matters and possesses significance.

Today, as an official 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization, Sincerely You Are Significant, Inc. operates from Eagles Landing Parkway in Stockbridge, GA, with individualized provider consultations offered by appointment at our Jonesboro location. Every initiative continues to be shaped by Bianca Reid's foundational values: Faith, People, Families, Community, and Impact.

Our Core Values

What We Stand For

Four core values guide everything we do — from the individuals we counsel to the families we strengthen and the community partnerships we build.

Empowering Individuals

Every person has unique gifts, potential, and purpose. We provide tools, resources, and encouragement to help each individual discover and live out their significance.

Strengthening Families

Families are the foundation of healthy communities. We support families through challenges and equip them to thrive with stability, connection, and renewed hope.

Building Community

True transformation happens in relationship. We cultivate spaces where people gather, connect, support one another, and build a stronger community together.

Advancing Kingdom Impact

Rooted in faith and guided by purpose, we pursue meaningful, lasting impact — honoring the vision written plainly for all who dare to run with it.
